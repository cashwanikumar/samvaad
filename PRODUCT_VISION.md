# Product Vision

## What We're Building

**AI-powered voice interview practice platform**

Where users can:
- Take realistic voice interviews with AI
- Get recorded and evaluated
- Track progress over time
- Follow learning paths from beginner to advanced

---

## Two Sides

### User Side
- Browse available interviews
- Take voice interviews (realtime)
- Review recordings (audio + transcript)
- See evaluation and feedback
- Track progress and improvements
- Follow structured learning paths

### Admin/Backoffice Side
- Create interview templates
- Add discussion guides
- Configure AI interviewer behavior
- Set evaluation criteria
- Manage interview catalog
- View analytics

---

## MVP (First Shippable Product)

**Single hardcoded interview:**
- System Design: "Design WhatsApp"
- User can take interview via voice
- Gets recording + transcript + evaluation
- No backoffice (we create templates manually)
- No user dashboard (just take interview)

**This proves:**
- Voice AI works
- Evaluation works
- Core experience is valuable

---

## Platform Vision (Long-term)

### Interview Marketplace
- Multiple interview types (frontend, backend, system design, behavioral)
- Different difficulty levels
- Different interviewer personas
- Custom company-specific interviews

### User Experience
- Personal dashboard
- Progress tracking
- Strengths/weaknesses analysis
- Recommended next interviews
- Learning paths

### Admin Experience
- Create interview templates
- Configure AI behavior per template
- Set custom evaluation rubrics
- Analytics on interview performance

### Learning Paths
- Structured progression (beginner → advanced)
- Career-focused tracks (frontend, backend, fullstack)
- Adaptive difficulty
- Personalized recommendations

## Competitive Advantage

**vs Traditional Interview Prep:**
- Practice anytime (no scheduling)
- Realistic voice interaction
- Immediate feedback
- Unlimited repetition

**vs Other AI Tools:**
- Voice-first (not chat)
- Structured evaluation
- Progress tracking
- Learning paths

**vs Human Mock Interviews:**
- Cheaper
- Available 24/7
- Consistent quality
- Detailed analytics
